Name: VI Simposio COLEVOL
Description: VI Simposio COLEVOL is a website guide to the COLEVOL-UNIVALLE 2017
  conference. COLEVOL is the Colombian Association of Evolutionary Biology. This
  conference was held at the University of Valle in Cali, Colombia and aimed to
  serve as a symposium on evolutionary biology. This conference also offered a
  course on the basics of comparative phylogenetic methods. Overall, the event
  "fostered interaction and connections between students from remote regions of
  the country and prestigious academic and technological centers of thought in
  Colombia and around the world". Read more on the website!
