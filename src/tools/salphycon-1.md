Description: salphycon is a front end for the phruta R package. It's meant to
  introduce the early steps of a phylogenetics pipeline (taxon and gene
  selection, sequence retrieval, curation, and alignment) in a classroom
  setting, while staying flexible enough for a quick real research workflow,
  before moving to heavier computational steps like tree inference.
