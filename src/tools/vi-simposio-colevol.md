Description: >-
  The Colombian Association of Evolutionary Biology (COLEVOL) is a non-profit
  organization committed to the development and promotion of the study,
  research, and dissemination of evolutionary biology in Colombia. Some of its
  strategies include the annual publication of the Colombian Bulletin of
  Evolutionary Biology, the organization of the Colombian Symposium on
  Evolutionary Biology every two years, and the establishment of regional
  working groups in various departments of Colombia.


  Since its inception as the Colombian Network of Evolutionary Biology, the Association has organized five symposia: Universidad de los Andes (2008), Universidad del Valle and ICESI (2009), Universidad de Antioquia and CES (2011), Universidad Central (2013), and Universidad del Quindío (2015). It also organized the IV Latin American School of Evolution (ELAEVO) in the city of Armenia (2015), in conjunction with the symposium of the same year.


  Traditionally, the Colombian Symposium on Evolutionary Biology has been a significant event for the development of Colombian science. Understanding evolutionary biology as an interdisciplinary field, these events have fostered interaction and connections between students from remote regions of the country and prestigious academic and technological centers of thought in Colombia and around the world. 


  COLEVOL's mission, focused on promoting Colombian science, has successfully fostered dialogue among academics interested in evolutionary biology, including undergraduate and graduate students, as well as researchers based both within and outside the country. We have also benefited from the participation and collaboration on research projects of world-renowned evolutionary biologists such as Peter and Rosemary Grant, Mohamed Noor, Douglas Futuyma, and Michael Arnold.


  The COLEVOL Regional Working Group of Valle del Cauca hopes to continue the previous achievements, through the organization of the **VI Colombian Symposium on Evolutionary Biology** and the **Introductory Course in Phylogenetic Comparative Methods** , to be held at the University of Valle, Cali, Colombia.


  The events within the framework of COLEVOL-UNIVALLE 2017 will bring together researchers interested in Evolutionary Biology at the national and international levels. Both the Course and the Symposium will give students and professors from different Colombian academic institutions the opportunity to interact and establish new academic contacts with researchers from prestigious universities around the world.


  We will have discussion sessions between the invited researchers and the participants to facilitate collaborations and agreements between institutions. These academic sessions are planned as working breakfasts and lunches with each of the speakers. Additionally, we are organizing other events to connect participants in a more informal setting (e.g., a farewell cocktail party).
