The lab manager has access to the department printer. We are only allowed to print department/research appropriate documents using this printer. If you need to print something related to your research project in the Data Diversity Lab, please get in contact with the lab manager to arrange for your documents to be printed. Documents like homework, and non-project related pages are not allowed to be printed, please avoid requesting these types of documents for printing. 

**Available Printer Locations**
1. Harvill room 409
2. The desktop in the conference room of the DDL is also configured.
