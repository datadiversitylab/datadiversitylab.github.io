**Summary Table**

| **Please DO Wear** | **Please DO NOT Wear** |
| --- | --- |
| Rich, deep, solid colors | Strong patterns or bold prints |
| Business professional attire (button up shirts or blouses, suit or sport coat, and minimal accessories) | Clothing with large logos or images |
| Stud earrings, pearls, diamonds or gemstones | Too many accessories |
| Clothing that fits you well | Short sleeves or sleeveless shirts |


-----


**Steps to follow before headshot:**

**1.	Only wear glasses that have minimal to no reflective coating. **
- Make sure the lenses are clean before your headshots.

**2. Jewelry should be tasteful and minimal.**

**3. Keep hair shiny and natural.**
- Only use products that enhance shine. 
- Do your hair in a style you typically wear.

**4.	Wear facial hair the way you normally would. **

**5.	Remember to try and dress to impress an ideal employer or to look like your boss.**
- Dress like you're trying to impress your ideal prospective collaborator.
- Choose moderately open necklines.
- Avoid low necklines you'll be tugging on.
- Avoid buttoned shirts that are too tight around your neck.

**6.	Do not wear tight patterns or tight textures.**
- Avoid small polka dots, pinstripes, small checks, and tight patterns of any kind.

**7.	Wear long sleeve or three-quarter length sleeves.**

**8.	Make sure clothing fit is right.**
- Avoid baggy or loose clothing, as it will make you look larger in photos.
- Avoid fabrics that are clingy or shiny.

**9.	Look put together and tidy.**
- Wear the right bra for the shirt you will wear. 
- Avoid logos of any kind.

**10.	Do not wear bright and light colors! Wear muted and dark colors instead. **
