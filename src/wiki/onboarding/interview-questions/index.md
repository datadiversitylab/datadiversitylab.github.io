**General questions**
* Why did you apply to this particular position?
* Briefly summarize the approach that you use to write a formal email
* How do you handle disagreement with a boss or a co-worker?
* Briefly mention three situations where there is clearly a sense of respect between coworkers. List three were there is none.
* What makes you feel supported in an academic environment? What about a non-academic one?
* You notice that a fellow lab member has left the group spaces of the lab very messy, how would you approach communicating this issue?
* There is a large disagreement during a lab meeting, how would you address this issue?
* Talk about how you organize projects, classes, your personal commitments, etc
* Based on your experience, what does the ideal amount of mentoring/guidance look for you?
* As a member of the DDL, you are expected to meet with Cristian, the lab's PI, at least once a week. Is this realistic for your schedule? Are you able to adapt to meet the lab's expectations of you?
* How do you plan on keeping information organized for projects, potential projects, etc.?
* Why do you think we should hire you?

**Postdocs**
* What has been your key realization about writing grants or papers? Anything that comes to mind?
* How would you deal with a grant rejection?
* As a postdoc in the lab, you are seen as a role model for younger students, how do you handle a leadership position? How do you plan on impacting other members of the lab through implementing our core values?
* What would you like to accomplish outside of the academy?
* How do you network at an academic conference?
* What would you like to learn during your time in the lab?
* How do you feel you will productively maintain submitting one paper per year as part of the requirements of this job position?
