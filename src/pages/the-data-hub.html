The University of Arizona Data Hub


The lab is developing a conceptual framework with structural implications for the academy and society. The data hub will focus on four areas.

1

The data hub will develop accessible resources for educators, with real-world examples from environmental science, sustainability, urban planning, health, ecology, climate science, and environmental justice. These show the societal implications of data science.

2

The data hub will review resources on ethics in machine learning and AI, including their use in these and other fields. The focus is on how data science tools are developed and used.

3

The data hub will critically evaluate materials used in data science education and place them in their historical and social context.

4

The data hub will mentor and support students and academics, particularly those from marginalized backgrounds, so they can succeed in data science and join projects that benefit from their perspectives.

The data hub is closely associated with the Center for Diverse Leadership in Science at the University of California, Los Angeles, led by Dr. Aradhna Tripati.
