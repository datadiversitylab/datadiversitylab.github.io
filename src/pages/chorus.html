chorus

Real-time, participatory polling for talks, workshops, and events. The room votes, suggests answers, and sees results live, no accounts, nothing stored permanently.

What it does

chorus was built for the moments when you want the room to actually participate, not just watch. Instead of a show of hands or a static survey, chorus lets a group vote, suggest their own answers, and see results update in real time. The organizer controls the pace: post a question, add answers of their own if needed, lock suggestions once discussion should move to voting, or push everyone to the next topic with a single click. Guests join with a four-character token, no sign-up, no app to install, and can suggest answers or vote from their own phone. Nothing is stored permanently, once the session ends, so does the data.

Running a session
1

Organizer starts a session and shares the four-character join token with the room.

2

Guests join from their own phone with the token, no account or app install needed.

3

The organizer posts a question. Guests vote or suggest their own answers.

4

Results update live. Lock suggestions, then move to the next topic whenever the room's ready.
