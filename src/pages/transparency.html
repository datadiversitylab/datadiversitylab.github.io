Transparency

We value transparency and openness. This section of our website discloses the salaries for most of the positions the lab fills. We believe it builds trust inside the lab and with the communities we belong to, whether online, at our institution, or in our city, state, and country. We also want people to see how their contributions (labor, taxes, donations, and grants) support effective research and the responsible use of the resources entrusted to us.
