Our work

The Data Diversity Lab started in 2023 at the University of Arizona. It is led by Dr. Cristian Román-Palacios, an ecologist and evolutionary biologist who uses bioinformatics and machine learning on big datasets to answer long-standing questions in biology.

Our papers on evolution, conservation, ecology, biostatistics, and climate change have appeared in PNAS, Proceedings B, Evolution, Journal of Biogeography, Evolution Letters, and Global Ecology and Biogeography.
