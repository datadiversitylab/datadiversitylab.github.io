In English

MacroData teaches R programming and phylogenetic comparative methods to undergraduate biology students in Colombia. The workshops are in person and in Spanish, and they are hosted by universities across the country. It is funded by a National Geographic grant to Cristian Román-Palacios: Supporting Colombian Undergraduates through Programming and Phylogenetic Comparative Methods in R.
