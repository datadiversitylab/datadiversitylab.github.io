Cristian Román-Palacios

Principal Investigator, Assistant Professor

Principal investigator Current member

Assistant Professor at the College of Information Science, UArizona and Early Career fellow at the Center for Diverse Leadership in Science, University of California, Los Angeles. Cristian's research is focused on the intersection between evolutionary biology, climate change, and applied machine learning. He is generally interested in answering questions in Evolutionary Biology and developing methods and tools for different fields.
